OriginalStake is a staked originality-challenge market for short creative text — settled by GenLayer validator consensus, not a moderator. A writer bonds GEN and submits a short piece (a tagline, a poem, a product name), which the contract embeds into its own on-chain vector index. Anyone else who thinks it's a near-copy of existing work can open a challenge by staking an equal counter-bond. A permissionless resolution step then deterministically finds the nearest prior submission via vector search, and a single consensus round compares the two texts — already stored in the contract, never fetched from outside — for substantial reuse of expression. Whoever's wrong loses their stake to the other side.

The problem: deciding whether new text substantially reuses someone else's expression, rather than just sharing a topic, is a judgment call no distance threshold or regex can make safely — and today that call is made by nobody, or by a single centralized moderator both sides have to trust blindly. This is deliberately not another escrow-with-a-delivery-step: there's no client or freelancer, no fixed payer role. Both sides independently put capital at risk before any judgment exists, and the evidence being judged already lives in the contract's own growing corpus.

How to use it: connect a wallet (generate one in-browser in seconds), submit a short text with a GEN bond, and anyone can preview the nearest on-chain neighbor before spending anything on a challenge. A challenger stakes an equal counter-bond; anyone can then trigger the permissionless resolution — a real multi-minute consensus round, not an instant result.

Measured result, run live on the deployed contract today, two genuinely different real outcomes in one clean test run (12/12 transactions SUCCESS/Accepted, zero errors): a near-verbatim spelling variant ("harbour" vs "harbor") was challenged and resolved SUBSTANTIALLY_SAME, with the contract's own stored reasoning: "The sentences are identical in wording and structure except for the trivial spelling variant 'harbor/harbour'." Bond moved to the challenger automatically. A second, unrelated submission (a business-revenue sentence) was challenged against the same corpus and correctly resolved DISTINCT: "One is a business revenue statement and the other is atmospheric lighthouse imagery, with no shared wording, structure, or expressive details."
